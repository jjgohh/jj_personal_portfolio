# Photo audit — every photograph in the company profile

Method: the 75 project photographs embedded in the profile PDF were extracted at their native resolution (the repeating tinted photo strip down the left edge of each page is page decoration, not project imagery, and is excluded). Each was inspected individually. Native pixel dimensions are the real constraint on how large a photo can be used: `p20 #2` at 650×720 px cannot fill a 1400 px-wide hero, whatever the layout wants.

**Headline: the photography is better than the brief feared, but it is unevenly distributed.** There are roughly **22 genuinely good photographs**, concentrated on residential work from 2016–2024 and on four industrial jobs. The company's two most recent corporate projects have no photographs at all. Every one of the 20 pre-2015 reference projects has exactly one photograph, and all of them are damaged in the same way (see §4).

## 4. The reference-project photographs (pp.46–63) — all 18 have the same problem

Every pre-2015 reference project has exactly one image, and each is a 715×715 px composite: a small photograph laid over a blue-tinted, enlarged crop of another photograph, with coloured bands showing above and below. Cropping to the inner photograph leaves roughly **715×430 px of real image**, which is enough for a small card and nothing more. They are also 12–24 years old, and several show men working without visible safety equipment (p55, p56, p62).

Recommendation: use them at card size only, on the directors' prior-experience section, cropped to the inner frame, and do not attempt to feature them. Four are worth keeping for the range they show:

- **p46** — a row of 59 two-storey link houses, Ulu Yam (the only volume-housing evidence in the whole profile)
- **p48** — five bungalows on a hillside street, College Heights Pajam (developer client)
- **p58** — a three-storey white building, Rawang
- **p63** — a green-and-cream four-storey building, Rawang

## 5. Photo gap list — what a reshoot should prioritise

The family can photograph completed buildings from the public road. In order:

1. **Balakong office building** (NX Transport Services, completed March 2024, RM3.63m) — a recent, corporate, nameable project with **no photograph at all**. Highest priority.
2. **Section 6 Petaling Jaya house** (completed June 2024, RM3.26m) — recent, no photograph. Private client, so exterior only and with the owner's agreement.
3. **Tanjung Malim business centre** (RM8.85m, the second-largest completed contract) — the three photos are weak; the exterior shots are shot into the sun across a road.
4. **Mantin supermarket** (RM8.12m, the largest completed contract) — one good photo exists; a second angle and an interior would carry a project page.
5. **Kuantan club house** (Sentoria) — the only photo is the weather-widget screenshot. If nothing better exists in the directors' phones, this project runs without a photo.
6. **Rawang temple** — good photos exist inside the collage; ask for the original files.
7. **Skudai factory warehouse** and **PJ warehouse** (both current) — ask the site supervisors to keep photographing monthly, with the phone's timestamp and watermark settings switched off.
8. **SJK(C) Kuang school block** and **Straits International School Rawang** — one more angle each.

Standing instruction for any new photograph: turn off every camera overlay (timestamp, watermark, weather, location), shoot landscape, shoot in the morning or late afternoon rather than at noon, include the whole building with some ground in front of it, and send the original file rather than a WhatsApp copy. WhatsApp re-compression is what has damaged several of the existing images.

## 6. Consequences for the design

- **The layout must not require a hero image wider than about 1000 px.** Only two photographs (p36 #1 and p38 #1) exceed 900 px, and neither is a natural homepage hero. A full-bleed photographic hero is therefore not available and should not be designed.
- **Cards must work at third-width**, because a third of the good photographs are only good enough for that size.
- **A photo-less project must look deliberate, not broken.** Four company-era projects have no usable image; every reference project has only a weak one. The design plan specifies a data-block treatment instead of a grey placeholder.
- **Every photograph needs a real caption** naming what it shows and its stage, because many of the good images are of work in progress rather than finished buildings, and an uncaptioned photo of a steel frame reads as someone else's project.

## 7. A note on the extracted files

The 75 photographs were extracted from the PDF to inspect them and are **deliberately not committed to this repository**. They come from a confidential tender document, several show private homes at identifiable addresses, and the decision on which may be published belongs to the family. Phase 3 will add only the approved images, cropped as recorded above, with EXIF stripped, under `src/assets/projects/<slug>/`.
