How we work

A director on site

We are a small company and we do not subcontract the supervision. A director is on site, our own two supervisors are on site, and our quantity surveyor measures the work in house.

Trades we have used for years

The plumber and the aluminium fabricator have worked with our directors since 2004, the tiler since 2007, the steel and roof-truss fabricator since 2016. When you ask who will be on your site, we can answer.

Priced off the drawings

Our project director is a qualified quantity surveyor. Send drawings and you get a price built from them, not a figure worked backwards from a budget.
