## D. Enquiry form — proposal for the family to correct

The brief asks what the form should ask so a lead can be qualified without three rounds of email. Proposal: six fields, all on one screen, nothing optional-looking that is actually needed.

1. **Name** and **phone** (phone required, email optional — this market replies by phone)
2. **What are you building?** — a select: new building · extension or renovation · demolish and rebuild · structure package only · interior fit-out · not sure yet
3. **Where?** — free text, town is enough
4. **Roughly what stage are you at?** — a select: just an idea · have drawings · have drawings and approval · tendering now
5. **Rough budget** — a select with wide bands, plus "prefer not to say"
6. **Anything else** — free text

Fields 2, 4 and 5 are the three that decide whether this is a job for GLC, and they are the three a phone call usually spends ten minutes establishing. Question for the family: is the budget band question acceptable, or does it put Malaysian private clients off?
