# Template — client permission to be named on the website

Send from the company email, one client at a time. Fill every `{…}`. Keep a copy of the reply with the project record. Do not publish the client's name or the contract value until a written "yes" is on file; record the answer in `content/open-questions.md` and flip `client_publish` / `value_publish` on the project.

---

**Subject:** Permission to list {Client company name} as a client — {short project name}

Dear {Contact name},

Global Land Consortium Sdn Bhd is preparing a company website that lists the projects we have completed. We would like to include the work we carried out for you:

- Project: {project title in plain English}
- Location: {town / area only}
- Period: {Month YYYY} – {Month YYYY}
- Our scope: {e.g. main contractor / RC structure sub-contract / interior fit-out}

May we have your permission to:

1. Name **{Client company name}** as the client for this project? — Yes / No
2. State the contract value (RM {value}) on the project page? — Yes / No
3. Use {number} photographs of the completed works taken by us? — Yes / No

We will not publish any other detail without your agreement, and we will remove the listing at any time on request. A short reply to this email is all we need.

Thank you for the opportunity to have worked with you.

Yours sincerely,
{Director name}
Director, Global Land Consortium Sdn Bhd (1089230-X)
