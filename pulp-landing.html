Fig. 03 — ResearchScientific Reports · 2026

The rarer half behaves differently — and often more powerfully.

In a 2026 peer-reviewed study, tocotrienols were roughly fifteen times more effective than α-tocopherol at inhibiting ferroptosis — a form of iron-driven cell damage caused by lipid oxidation — measured in human cells in the laboratory.

Yang et al., "Tocotrienols exhibit superior ferroptosis inhibition over tocopherols," Scientific Reports 16:4497 (2026) · in-vitro, human cells · EC50 0.12 μM vs 2.0 μM. This is early laboratory research on tocotrienols in general, not a claim that PULP treats, cures or prevents any disease.

Fig. 04 — DeliveryBio-Enhanced

Getting it in is half the battle.

Tocotrienols are a viscous, poorly water-soluble oil — famously hard to absorb. PULP is built on a patented self-emulsifying delivery system designed to fix exactly that, so more of what's in the softgel actually reaches you.

Why it earns a daily place.

01

Skin barrier

Vitamin E is a fat-soluble antioxidant that helps defend skin against the everyday oxidative stress of sun, heat and city air — the kind that quietly adds up.

02

Cellular defence

Fat-soluble antioxidants work inside cell membranes, helping protect cells from oxidative stress where water-soluble antioxidants can't reach.

03

Start early

The best time to support your body was years ago. The second best is today. PULP is built for the generation that future-proofs, rather than fixes.

A Note From The Founder

Why I built PULP around a fruit the world overlooks.

Here in Malaysia, the oil palm is part of the landscape. What most people never hear is that its fruit produces one of the most complete forms of vitamin E on earth — the full spectrum of tocotrienols the rest of the world mostly sets aside for a single synthetic molecule.

It never sat right with me that the best version of this nutrient comes from home, yet almost no one here makes it the hero. So we did. PULP is one thing, done completely — for people who would rather start early than catch up later.
